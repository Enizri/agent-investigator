## Summary

CWE-915 is improperly controlled modification of object attributes, often called mass assignment. A profile update that accepts a role field is the example.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-915, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
