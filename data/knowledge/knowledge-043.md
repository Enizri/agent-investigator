## Summary

CWE-732 is incorrect permission assignment. A bucket policy that allows anonymous object listing is an example. Mitigation without a verification record stays unverified.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-732, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
