## Summary

CWE-284 is improper access control, the parent of several broken access control cases. Use it when the incident did not settle on a more specific id such as CWE-639 or CWE-22.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-284, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
