## Summary

CWE-308 is use of single-factor authentication where the policy requires more. An MFA prompt storm during a vendor test can be noise. Read the alert disposition.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-308, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
