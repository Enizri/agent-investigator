## Summary

CWE-16 is configuration. It is a broad parent for security misconfiguration, including debug routes and loose platform defaults. Use a more specific CWE when the incident has one.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-16, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
