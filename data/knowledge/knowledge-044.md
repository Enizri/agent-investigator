## Summary

CWE-613 is insufficient session expiration. Sessions that survive password reset fit this weakness. A later regression reopens the incident even if a September verification once passed.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-613, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
