## Summary

CWE-326 is inadequate encryption strength. TLS 1.0 and short keys are examples. The evidence is the listener or key configuration, not a user complaint.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-326, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
