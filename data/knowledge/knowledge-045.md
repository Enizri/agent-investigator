## Summary

CWE-942 is an overly permissive cross-domain whitelist. Reflected CORS origins on internal routes are the case. The browser's error page is not the authority for whether the policy was fixed.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-942, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
