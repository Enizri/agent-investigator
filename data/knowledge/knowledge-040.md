## Summary

CWE-840 is business logic errors. It labels insecure design such as a missing refund approver. Do not expect a WAF rule to be the primary evidence.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-840, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
