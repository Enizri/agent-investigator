## Summary

CWE-489 is active debug code. A billing debug route that exposes invoice totals is a misconfiguration with this weakness. Leaving the route mounted after a rollback reopens the issue.

## Prevention

When a record cites CWE-489, retrieve this note for the definition and then verify the live status in SQLite. The CWE page does not know whether this company's incident is open.
